Hradčanské náměstí

In één oogopslag

Hradčanské náměstí is het historic square directly west of Prague Castle and the gateway to the Hradčany district. The square is surrounded by aristocratic palaces and religious buildings and forms one of the most impressive ensembles on the castle hill.

Het verhaal

Hradčany developed around the castle as a settlement of people serving the court and church. After fires and rebuilding, especially in the Baroque period, the district received the grand palaces and religious complexes that still determine its appearance. From the square, the route can continue towards Loreto, Nový Svět and Strahov.

Wist je dat?

Loreto was founded in 1626 and became an important pilgrimage site. Its famous monstrance, known as the Prague Sun, is decorated with 6,222 diamonds. The Santa Casa is connected with a religious legend; that legend should be distinguished from the documented history of the building.

Persoon uit het verhaal

The Schwarzenberg family is closely connected with the monumental palace on the square. The palace reflects the wealth and status of the Bohemian aristocracy in the 16th century.

Wat zie je hier?

On and around the square you can see the Schwarzenberg Palace, Loreto and a sequence of historic aristocratic buildings. A short detour leads to the small streets of Nový Svět; further west lies Strahov Monastery.

Praktische informatie

The square is a good transition point between Prague Castle and the quieter Hradčany/Strahov area. The surrounding streets are mostly uphill or downhill and can be cobbled.
